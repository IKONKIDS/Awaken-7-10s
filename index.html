What should my child bring to a session? +

Please ensure your child comes prepared with the following:

  • A labelled water bottle (especially on hot days!)
  • Any required ear defenders (we have a limited supply)

Please do not send your child to a session with snacks. We will be providing this and monitoring for allergies.

How does independant collection work? +

If you choose to allow your child to leave independently after the morning session, we will tag their wristband and mark this on the register. At the end of the session, we will then check this and allow them to leave without you needing to come and hand in a collection card.
