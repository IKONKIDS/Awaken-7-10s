What should my child bring to a session? +

What to Bring:

  • Labeled Water Bottle – Essential for staying hydrated outdoors (especially on warm days!).
  • Ear Defenders – Please pack these if your child requires them, as our on-site backup supply is limited.

Important Guidelines:

  • Snacks: Please do not pack extra snacks. We provide snacks and directly monitor for allergies.
  • Toys & Electronics: Toys and gadgets should not be brought into a session as they can be a distraction and easily get lost or damaged.

If you have any questions, please speak to a member of our registration team.

How does independent collection work? +

If you have opted to allow your child to leave independently after the morning session:

  • At Registration: We will mark this on the register and tag their wristband.
  • At End of Session: We will check their wristband and allow them to leave without you needing to present a collection card.

Changed your mind? Please speak to a member of our registration team.

My child has additional needs. How will they be supported in sessions? +

We aim for full inclusion, and our sessions are designed with this in mind. Children stay with their group where leaders provide support. If they need a break, our low-sensory SEN Zone at the back offers ear defenders, fidgets, a sensory tent, and a team member to support them before rejoining the group.

If there is anything specific we should know about your child’s needs, please speak to us at registration. Please note that while we support children within the 7–10s age range, we are unable to accommodate children outside this age bracket in our sessions.

While we cannot offer 1-to-1 support, we will work with you to help them access our sessions. Please keep your phone on during sessions in case we need to contact you to collect them early if they are struggling.

(Note: Ear defenders are limited, so please bring your own if possible. Small comfort items are welcome if they aren't fragile or overly distracting.)

What is the Peg Challenge? +

Each child receives a coloured peg with a simple mission: find a group leader outside of the session and clip the peg onto them without getting caught! Once clipped, snap a quick photo, upload it using the link above, and earn points for your team.

Safety Guidelines:

  • Please only clip a peg onto someone if you are absolutely certain they are a member of the 7–10s Kids Team.
  • We ask that a grown-up takes and uploads all of the challenge photos.
My child has a sibling who is slightly outside the 7–10 age bracket. Can they join the session too? +

Unfortunately, no. All of our sessions, teaching, and activities are designed specifically for the 7 to 10 age group. We aren't able to include children outside of this age range, and we prioritise maintaining safe leader-to-child ratios.

We are very happy to direct you to the right venue for your child's age group. We appreciate your honesty and understanding.

How can I join the 7–10s Kids Team for 2027? +

We would love to speak to you about joining the team next year! Serving on the 7–10s Kids Team is a fantastic opportunity to invest in our children's faith and help them grow in their understanding of God (…and you’ll receive a free or discounted ticket to Awaken 2027!).

We have a range of volunteer opportunities, from serving as a group leader or supporting our media and tech teams.

For more information and to get signed up, please ask to speak to Jacob Yeomans.

Who do I contact if I have a question? +

This team and venue is led by Jacob Yeomans, Kids Pastor of IKON Church.

Please speak to Jacob or share your query with our registration team and we’ll be able to direct you.
